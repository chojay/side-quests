## Ingredients

### Sauce Base

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 간장 (soy sauce) | 50g (6 spoons) | - |
| 미린 (mirin) | 40g (7 spoons) | - |
| 설탕 (sugar) | 30g (½ spoon) | Split: half in sauce, half for caramelizing onion |
| 물엿 (corn syrup) | 20g (~2 spoons) | - |
| 매실액 (plum concentrate) | 10g (1.5–2 spoons) | - |
| [[연두]] (Yondu) | 10g (~1.75 spoons) | - |
| 물 (water) | 100g (16 spoons) | - |
| 참기름 (sesame oil) | 15g (2 spoons) | Added at the very end |

### Meat

- **Beef**: 400g, thick cut (NOT thin bulgogi-sliced meat)
  - Preferred: 치마살/안창살 (skirt steak), even thickness, balanced fat
  - Cut into big steak-sized chunks (cut after cooking, not before)

### Vegetables & Aromatics

- 양파 (onion): half, cut following its natural curve
- 파 (green onion): 1–2 stalks (one bite-size, one diagonal for garnish)
- 마늘 (garlic): 3–4 whole cloves (crush 2, mince 1)
- 생강 (ginger): pinky-sized piece (2 thin slices + mince the rest)
- 새송이버섯 (king oyster mushroom): sliced thin
- 팽이버섯 (enoki mushroom): to taste

## Method

### Prep Phase

1. **Make the sauce base:** Combine soy sauce, mirin, corn syrup, plum concentrate, [[연두]], and HALF the sugar. Add 2 crushed garlic cloves, 2 ginger slices. Warm gently. Strain through a sieve.
2. **Prep vegetables:** Cut onion, green onion (two ways), mushrooms. Mix minced garlic + ginger.
3. **Prep meat:** Cut into big steak-sized chunks. Do NOT cut bite-size.

### Cooking Phase (~6 minutes)

4. **Preheat pan extremely hot.** Add oil.
5. **Sear meat (Layer 3, Maillard):** High heat, sear hard. Keep interior rare/medium-rare. Transfer into sauce container.
6. **DO NOT WASH THE PAN.** The fond is essential.
7. **Cook onion + deglaze (Layers 4 & 5):** Add onion + remaining sugar to pan. Onion's moisture deglazes the fond.
8. **Add aromatics (Layers 6 & 7):** Green onion, garlic-ginger mix. Add HALF water to deglaze. Transfer to meat container.
9. **Cook mushrooms:** Add oil, fry mushrooms. Add remaining water to deglaze. Transfer to container.

### Resting Phase

10. **Rest 15 minutes** (up to 1 hour). Meat proteins relax and absorb marinade.

### Finishing Phase

11. **Cut meat** into bite-size pieces. Inside should still be pink.
12. **Reheat pan to very hot (Layer 8):** Pour everything in. Smoky/charred flavor from contact. Reduce to medium.
13. **Add black pepper** (late, as it burns easily).
14. **Add sesame oil right before plating (Layer 9).**
15. **Plate + garnish (Layer 10):** Fresh diagonal green onion + sesame seeds.

## Tips

- The pan must be extremely hot; the recipe fails without sufficient heat
- All soy sauces have different saltiness, so adjust through experience
- Do not cut meat bite-size before searing (same reason you don't cut steak before cooking)
- Use a scale for accuracy; spoon sizes vary by household

## Science & Key Insights

- **Maillard Reaction:** Traditional bulgogi marinates first, preventing browning. This recipe sears FIRST, then marinates during resting.
- **Deglazing:** Western technique applied to Korean cooking; the fond contains concentrated umami flavor.
- **Sugar Split:** Half in sauce (sweetness), half for onion caramelization.
- **Sesame Oil Last:** Aromatic oils lose fragrance on high heat, so add right before plating.
