# Early Research Notes (Dec 2025 - Feb 2026)

Part of the [Gaggiuino build research export](../README.md). These are the original community-sourced research notes on the [Gaggiuino](https://gaggiuino.github.io/) espresso machine mod, written while planning and running a Gaggiuino build on a **Gaggia Classic Evo Pro (RI9380/49)** with a DF64 grinder. Compiled from the official Gaggiuino docs, the project Discord, Reddit (r/gaggiaclassic, r/espresso), Home-Barista, Coffee Forums UK, YouTube practitioners, and vendor documentation. Several were later consolidated into the canonical guides under [machine/](../machine/) and [troubleshooting/](../troubleshooting/); these originals are kept for their fuller sourcing detail.

These files were exported from a personal Obsidian vault: frontmatter removed, wiki links flattened to plain text, and personal purchase details stripped. Source citations and confidence ratings are preserved inline.
