Visible edge, true scale

The one difference you see in the installed vent: the plate edge at the floor line. 1.6 and 2.0 mm are nearly indistinguishable by eye; 4.5 mm reads almost 3× heavier.

How each stays strong - cross-section, true scale

Thinner skin is bought with a denser spine grid underneath (span ∝ t²): 28 → 14 → 9 → 6 mm spacing. The 2.8 and 4.5 mm sections show the magnet-pocket bumps; pockets are deleted on 2.0 and 1.6.
